# 🧼 LinkCleaner

### 🕵️‍♂️ Your links went shopping… we sent them home clean.

Ever copied a TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat, or Pinterest link and ended up with a giant mess of tracking stuff attached to it?

Yeah. Nobody asked for all that nonsense. 🫠

**LinkCleaner** takes your messy links, removes unnecessary tracking/query parameters, and gives you a nice, clean link back. ✨

No detective work required. 🔎

## 🤖 Meet the Telegram Bot

You can also send your links directly to the **LinkCleaner Telegram Bot**.

Send it a supported link → it does its little cleaning magic → you get the fresh version back. 🫧

Supported platforms:

* 📸 Instagram
* 👻 Snapchat
* 🎵 TikTok
* 📌 Pinterest

---

## 🧽 What does it actually do?

You send:

```text
https://example.com/something?utm_source=my_friend&utm_campaign=please_leave_me_alone
```

LinkCleaner gives you:

```text
https://example.com/something
```

Much better. Less clutter. Same destination. 🧼
